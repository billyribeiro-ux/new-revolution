## 2. Executive Summary

Choosing a live trading room in 2026 comes down to three structural realities that marketing highlight reels hide:

1. **Underlying Liquidity & Slippage:** Low-float penny stock chatrooms suffer immediate crowd front-running when hundreds of members hit market buy together; cash-settled SPX/XSP index options and SPY/QQQ ETFs absorb institutional volume cleanly.
2. **Chart Dependency:** Indicator-heavy rooms often monetize $500–$1,500+ proprietary add-ons, whereas **The Move Prior to The Move™** teaches 100% indicator-free Monthly-to-1-Minute price structure on clean brokerage charts.
3. **Post-PDT Small-Account Risk Governance:** Under **FINRA Regulatory Notice 26-10 / Amended Rule 4210(d)(2)** (effective June 4, 2026 through October 20, 2027), small margin accounts ($2,000+) and T+1 cash accounts ($500+) require explicit pre-trade binary invalidation stops and position sizing.
