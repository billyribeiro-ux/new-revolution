## 4. Findings and their status

- **G5 (out-of-range and malformed `page`) fixed in Phase 1a.** `?page=999`, `?page=abc` and every page beyond the last now return 404 with no cache headers on the index, category and tag archives. Proved by `src/routes/blog/blog-pagination.spec.ts` (mutation-checked) and the tables above.
- **G15 (cacheable 404 for unknown categories and tags) fixed in Phase 1a.** `setPublicReadCache` now runs only after the page is known to exist.
- **Pages 2+ are indexable with their own canonical** (decision 3), proved with 13 fixture posts. The probe cannot show it because the mock data has three posts.
- **G12 narrowed:** no duplicate or missing title, description, canonical or robots tag on any probed template. Open Graph article tags, `og:image` dimensions and author tags still need a field-level check.
- **G1 partly addressed:** the stale window shrank from 5 minutes + 24 hours to 5 + 1 minutes, and every public response now carries a `Vercel-Cache-Tag`. Purging itself is not connected (needs authorization).
