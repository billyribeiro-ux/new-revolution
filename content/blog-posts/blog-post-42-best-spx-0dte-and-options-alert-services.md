## 2. Why Raw "Buy Strike X Now" Alerts Fail Working Traders

On short-dated and 0DTE index options, high gamma causes contracts to reprice 20%–35% in seconds. A serious options alert service must publish a **5-point pre-trade structural blueprint** before price enters the zone:

1. **Higher-Timeframe Supply/Demand Location** (Monthly → 4-Hour pure price structure, zero indicators)
2. **Conditional Confirmation Trigger** (exact candle reaction required before entry)
3. **Engineered Contract Structure** (cash-settled SPX/XSP Section 1256 single-leg vs. defined-risk vertical spread)
4. **Tiered Scaling Targets** (Target 1, Target 2, runner rule)
5. **Binary Underlying Hard Stop** (mechanical exit with zero averaging down into 0DTE theta decay)
